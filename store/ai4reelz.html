Pro-Tips for Cinematic AI Video

Strict prompting rules to achieve clean, professional results and avoid generation breaks.

1. Bind Actions with Time

Use the word "while" to specify simultaneous actions so the model parses time correctly.
Ex: "A drone flies over a mountain while the sun sets."

2. One Verb Per Subject

Stacking multiple consecutive actions on a single subject breaks the generation. Give every subject one clear action, and split secondary actions into a separate clip.

3. Prioritize Concrete Nouns

Vague adjectives get poor results. Use highly descriptive, concrete nouns for framing, style, and lighting.
Ex: "Anamorphic lens, 35mm, neon-lit cyberpunk alleyway."

4. Always Upscale & Enhance

Most raw AI video exports in low resolution. Agent MARS automatically routes exports through AI video upscalers to boost quality to 4K at 60 or 120 FPS.
