📱 Google Cardboard & Mobile VR

  • The Setup: Slide a compatible smartphone into a low-cost cardboard or plastic shell equipped with focal lenses.
  • How to Access: Download VR-enabled apps (like YouTube VR or Google Arts & Culture) from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
  • The Experience: Best for 360-degree videos and basic, stationary 3DoF (Three Degrees of Freedom) experiences.

🎮 Tethered Console & PC VR

  • The Setup: HMDs like the PlayStation VR2 or older PC headsets (HTC Vive, Oculus Rift) connect directly to a gaming console or powerful PC via cables or dedicated wireless links.
  • How to Access: Launch VR platforms directly on your system, such as SteamVR, PlayStation Store, or the Oculus PC App.
  • The Experience: High-fidelity graphics, low latency, and full 6DoF (Six Degrees of Freedom) motion tracking for complex gaming and simulations.

SteamVR Setup Guide

1

Install Steam & SteamVR

Download the Steam client from store.steampowered.com. Once installed, go to your Library, search for "SteamVR", and click Install.

2

Connect Your Headset

Plug your HMD (e.g., HTC Vive, Valve Index, or older Rift) into your PC's USB and DisplayPort/HDMI connections. Ensure base stations are powered on.

3

Run Room Setup

Launch SteamVR. Click the hamburger menu (☰) in the top left of the SteamVR window and select "Run Room Setup". Follow the on-screen prompts to define your play area.

Troubleshooting Tip:

If your headset displays a "Headset Disconnected" error, try unplugging and replugging the link box, or restart the SteamVR service via the Steam settings menu.
