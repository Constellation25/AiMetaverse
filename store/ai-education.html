🎓 AI Education Paths by Role

Major technology leaders and institutions now offer comprehensive, specialized training paths tailored to different learning needs.

Students & Lifelong Learners

Cut your learning time significantly by using a structured framework (Goal, Research, Priming, Comprehension, and Implementation). Tools like Google NotebookLM allow you to upload documents, generate instant study guides, and create custom self-quizzes.

Educators & Teachers

Programs like the Google AI for Educators course and the Microsoft AI-Powered Teaching path teach educators how to safely use tools like Gemini and Copilot to design dynamic lesson plans and differentiate reading levels.

Professionals & Small Business

The Google AI Professional Certificate and OpenAI Academy Courses focus on workflow thinking—breaking large business operations into smaller steps that AI can automate to increase team efficiency.

🛠️ Strategic Framework to Learn AI in 3 Months

If you want to become fully AI-literate, adopt a phased approach to build your fluency and accelerate your AI journey.

1

Phase 1: Foundations (Habit Building)

Swap your daily search engine queries out for an LLM (like Claude, Gemini, or ChatGPT) to practice natural language prompting.

2

Phase 2: Workflow Thinking (Deconstructing Tasks)

Break large projects down into distinct phases instead of throwing a single, massive prompt at the AI.

3

Phase 3: Creative Remixing (Tool Integration)

Combine different generative tools (text, image, data analysis) to discover unique solutions and workflows.

We’re preparing students to thrive in a world where AI is everywhere.

We envision a world where our education system evolves to better meet the new demands of a world transformed by AI. This requires a national effort to bring together education partners, school systems, and other stakeholders in the K12 ecosystem to work collaboratively toward a clear, shared goal.

We empower educators and school leaders.

aiEDU utilizes research-based strategies and educational psychology frameworks to create equitable training experiences and curriculum for teachers of Computer Science, Math, English, Social Studies, Science, and CTE; a wide range of administrators; and nonprofits. Our trainings are designed to help build AI Readiness and empower students with AI literacy.
