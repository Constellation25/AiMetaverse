🏗️ Commz Platform Architecture

A hybrid open-source + API stack designed to keep margins high and latency ultra-low.

Telephony & Dialing

Twilio API or Vonage for reliable PSTN dialing, Answering Machine Detection (AMD), and call recording.

STT (Speech-to-Text)

Deepgram Nova-2 (~300ms latency) or local Whisper.cpp via Termux for $0 cost.

The Brain (LLM)

Qwen2.5-72B (via API) or Qwen2.5-7B (local via Ollama) for sub-second reasoning and objection handling.

TTS (Text-to-Speech)

ElevenLabs for premium human-like voice, or Coqui TTS / Piper (open-source, $0 cost, local).

Orchestration

n8n or custom Python FastAPI webhooks connecting CRM triggers directly to the voice pipeline.

⚙️ How Commz Works

From trigger to close, our core mechanics handle the heavy lifting.

1. Triggers & Automation

  • CRM Sync: New leads added via web form or Mercury agent scraping.
  • n8n Workflow: Instantly triggers a Commz webhook with lead data.
  • Dynamic Scripting: Generates a personalized opening hook based on source and company data.

2. Real-Time Execution

  • Smart Dialing: AMD instantly identifies human vs. voicemail.
  • Voicemail Bypass: Leaves hyper-realistic, pre-approved AI voicemails and logs to CRM.
  • Live Conversation: Qualifies leads (BANT) and handles initial objections seamlessly.
  • Live Sync: Transcribes every word and pushes key data back to CRM in real-time.

3. Human Handoff

  • Intent Detection: LLM detects high buying intent or complex questions.
  • Seamless Transfer: "Let me connect you directly with our director..."
  • Context Payload: Live rep receives full transcript, sentiment analysis, and extracted data before saying "Hello."
